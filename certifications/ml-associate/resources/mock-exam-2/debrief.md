## Study plan by miss count

| Missed | Status | Study plan |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **0–7** (≥ 85 %) | Excellent | Quick review of any missed topic; you're exam-ready |
| **8–14** (70–84 %) | Likely passing | Identify the 2–3 weakest sections; spend 1–2 hours per section re-reading the topic folder |
| **15–21** (55–69 %) | On the bubble | Block 1–2 weeks of focused study on the weakest sections; re-attempt the other mock |
| **22–28** (40–54 %) | Not ready yet | 2–3 weeks of comprehensive review; work through each topic folder in order |
| **29+** (< 40 %) | Significant gap | Restart from shared/fundamentals/. Plan a 4–6 week cycle |
